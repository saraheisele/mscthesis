\section{Abstract}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item three reproducible waveform classes were identified in the large
    long-term dataset;
    \item overall and wide-pulse activity showed a clear feeding-related
    response, while the strongest increase in special-pulse activity occurred
    during a distinct late-2025 period;
    \item this late-2025 increase coincided with a shift in coarse space use
    and unusual social observations, providing evidence consistent with,
    but not demonstrating, a social or reproductive state.
\end{enumerate}
