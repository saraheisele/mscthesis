\section{Acknowledgements}

First and foremost, I would like to thank my supervisors, \textbf{Jan Benda and Patrick Weygoldt}. 
Jan, thank you for being one of the most open-minded professors I have had the pleasure of working with. 
I am incredibly grateful for the freedom you gave me to explore, to follow my curiosity, and to develop my own scientific enthusiasm. 
You always matched and encouraged my excitement when I came up with a good idea, while using the less great ideas to teach me valuable lessons. 
That combination gave me the confidence to explore while learning how to think critically about my own ideas.

Patrick, thank you for always being reliable, structured and for creating an environment where I felt safe to ask any question I had. 
Communicating with you was always easy, and that made working on this thesis so much more enjoyable. 
Thank you for teaching me from scratch how to turn an idea into a finished recording tool, and for showing me that a good idea is usually much more doable than I initially think. 
Thank you also for becoming a friend while somehow managing to remain a great boss. 
I admire your scientific work so much, and it was a pleasure to be a part of it. 
I learned a lot from watching you work and develop new ideas, and I'm impressed by the level of care and perfection you put into everything.

I would like to thank the \textbf{PROMOS programme and the Teufel Stiftung} for their financial support, which made it possible for me to spend a month in Brazil. 
I am deeply grateful for the opportunity this funding provided.

Thanks also go to \textbf{David de Santana (Smithsonian), Douglas Bastos (INPA), and Nonato (FloNa)} for enabling our expedition to Brazil, helping us with every  personal and scientific question along the way, 
and generously sharing your knowledge and expertise with us.

I am equally grateful to everyone in the \textbf{Benda lab}, with a special mention to \textbf{Yvonne, Jan Grewe, and Uli}. 
Your everyday contributions and support make this lab the great place it is. 
\textbf{Lena, Jona, and Patrick}, my office mates, thank you for the countless tea sessions, the emotional support, the laughs, and for getting genuinely invested in my questions whenever I needed help. 
I am especially grateful that I got to go through the experience of doing a masters thesis alongside my love, Lena. 
Knowing that I could count on you to simply be there and be yourself made me happy every day.

A very special thank you goes to \textbf{Louis}, who always had my back throughout this whole process. 
Thank you for talking through code problems with me, recommending music, being my cheerleader, supporting me by picking up the slack at home, and cooking the best meals so I did not have to live on Mensa food. 
Knowing that I had you in my corner gave me so much security and freedom to put my energy into this thesis.

Finally, I want to thank \textbf{Peter Bartsch from the Museum für Naturkunde Berlin} for taking such great care of the eels that provided the database for this thesis. 
The data were incredible to work with, and I greatly appreciate the work that went into making this research possible.

My thesis was so special because I was able to do hardware construction, fieldwork in Brazil and analysis all by myself. 
To make this happen it took a lot and I am beyond grateful to all of you for contributing to making me a Master of Science.
